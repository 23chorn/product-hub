# Feature Marketing: In-App Messaging & Trade Chat — xCube

## Positioning Statement

**For** active retail traders who discuss setups on Discord and WhatsApp before trading on xCube, **the Chat feature** is a real-time trading community built into the app, **that** eliminates the context-switch between conversation and execution — so traders can go from idea to order in one tap, not six minutes.

## Core Value Props

### 1. Topic channels, not DMs
Join channels organised by ticker, sector, or strategy. Jump into #nvda-bulls at earnings or create your own #macro-watch channel. The community comes to you — you don't have to find it on Discord.

### 2. Ticker cards: from idea to order in one tap
When a trader in the channel shares a live AAPL card, you see the current price and change — and you tap once to open the order screen. That's the gap between "I should buy this" and "I bought this."

### 3. Live, not later
Messages arrive in under 500ms. Trading ideas are time-sensitive. This isn't a news feed or a forum — it's the conversation happening right now as the market moves.

### 4. Compliant by design
Every message is moderated before delivery. All conversations are retained for 7 years. Pump-and-dump signals are automatically flagged. This is a community you can trust.
