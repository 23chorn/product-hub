## 3. Feature Benefits (3-up grid)

### ⚡ Instant alerts
Get push notifications within 30 seconds of your target price being hit — on any tradable equity or ETF.

### 🎯 Trade with intention
Unlike limit orders, alerts don't execute automatically. You stay in control. Get notified, then decide.

### 📋 Watchlist-connected
Alerts sync with your watchlist. Add a stock to your watchlist and set an alert in one tap — no context switching.

## 4. App Store Copy

### What's New (release notes)
**Price Alerts — Know the moment it moves**

Set price alerts on any stock or ETF in your watchlist. We'll send you a push notification the instant your target is crossed — above or below. No limit orders. No accidental fills. Just information when you need it.

- Create alerts directly from a position or watchlist item
- Above/below threshold alerts for equities and ETFs
- View and manage all alerts in one place
- Alert history kept for 30 days

Real-time price alerts for your watchlist. Get notified when a stock hits your target — in under 30 seconds.

## 7. Creative Direction

**Visual language:** Clean, confident, data-forward. No aggressive red/green. Show the alert notification UI in-context on the device (not isolated on white).

**Key screenshot:** Alert creation flow (2 steps) → push notification landing on lock screen → trade execution.

**Video (15s):** User adds stock to watchlist → sets alert → goes about their day → phone lights up with alert → opens app → executes trade. End frame: "Price Alerts. Now in TradeEasy."

**Tone:** Informed, calm, empowering. Investor, not gambler. Never "profit" language. Regulatory-safe.
