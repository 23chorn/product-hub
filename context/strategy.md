# Strategic Overview

## North star

Make confident, informed investing the default for the GCC's emerging retail investor — 
the platform that turns first-time investors into self-directed, active participants
in regional and global markets.

## North Star Metric

Monthly active investors — users who log in AND execute at least 
one informed action (trade, watchlist update, portfolio review) 
per month.

## Business Model

**Direct (B2C):** xCube mobile and web apps - retail investors in UAE

**Infrastructure (B2B):** White label mobile app and API-as-a-service — 
licensed to financial institutions, fintechs, and regional brokers who 
want to offer investment capabilities without building core infrastructure

## Current period: Q2–Q3 2026

### OKRs

### Roadmap themes this period

- Offer white label and developer solutions to enable third parties to use xCube Infrastructure as a broker
- Expand into passive investment strategies via xCube Access — pre-built, professionally managed DFM/ADX equity portfolios that retail investors subscribe to with a cash allocation, targeting users who want market exposure without active trading. First product in the UAE market offering mobile-first passive local equity strategies. Partnership with Shuaa Capital for strategy management and rebalancing.
- Improve methods to deposit and withdraw funds from the app, including recurring payments via Credit/Debit card and reducing time for fund transfer to and from users bank account. Current wait is T+1 and requires manual process from internal teams

## Explicit non-priorities (Q2–Q3)

- Crypto trading — regulatory complexity in the GCC; not in scope until core equity product is stable
- Web platform feature parity — mobile is the primary channel; web is secondary and maintained but not being expanded this half
- GCC market expansion beyond UAE (other than regulatory groundwork) — premature until UAE product quality OKRs are met

## Constraints (budget, timeline, regulatory)

- iOS and Android must ship simultaneously — no platform-first releases
- All trading flows support both English and Arabic — Arabic localization follows the standard, established translation process (see process.md → "Localization & translation"); it is covered delivery work and does not need to be raised per feature
- SCA regulatory compliance
