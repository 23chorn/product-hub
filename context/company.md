# Company Overview Context

## Mission & Vision

The mission of xCube is to act as the global access point to the middle east markets. In particular with a focus on UAE & other GCC nations. Its vision is to bring innovation to the region through first to market features that traders have come to expect across developed markets (US & Europe) and also bring region specific innovation such as Shariah compliant investing options.1

## Product Description

The primary product for xCube is a IOS/Android mobile application which serves as the gateway to the services. They have a number of other product lines including an internal & external advanced desktop trading application. A web version of the app is now live, a small set of users are using with a bigger marketing push coming soon. xCube is also expanding into offering their services as a Saas White label application and API-as-a-product through API connectivity to the services.

## Target User Segments

The Target user segments include UAE locals looking to invest in local markets (DFM and ADX exchanges) and also global users looking for access to the regions markets.

## Market & Competitive position

xCube is in a prime position to run as the primary access point to UAE and other GCC markets. Competitors do not have full access to both exchanges. xCube is also the first to offer Securities Lending & Borrowing on UAE equities as well as access to IPO subscriptions and basic risk controls such as Stop Loss.

Competitors include the main retail banking units such as FAB and ENBD who offer brokerage services via their apps, however this is not their primary focus so in general the features and UX is lacking compared to a dedicated trading app like xCube. International platforms such as Sarwa and StashAway operate in the UAE but focus on global ETF portfolios and do not offer direct DFM/ADX equity exposure. No competitor currently offers a mobile-first, curated passive strategy product built on DFM/ADX local equities.

## Key differentiators

1. Full access to DFM and ADX exchanges
2. Close working relationships to the DFM & ADX
3. Dubai government backed entity
4. First to market with a series of products for the local markets as outlined in the previous section
5. Access to professional advisors directy through the app for high net worth individuals

## Business model overview

Generate revenue through commissions on trade executions, deposits. Expanding into recurring management fee revenue via passive investment strategies (1% annual fee on assets under management in strategy sub-accounts, collected monthly).
